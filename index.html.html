Steps to qualify

✓
Confirm income eligibility Do first
Your $95k income (~112% AMI) earns you a $40,000 Zone A grant. Household income includes all adults 18+. Max to qualify at all: 150% AMI ($127,650 for single).
✓
Complete HUD-certified homebuyer education Required
Must be done before applying. Look for NHS Chicago or HUD-approved counselors. You'll receive a certificate needed for the application.
→ Find HUD counselors near Chicago
✓
Get mortgage pre-approval Required
A pre-approval letter is required to apply. At $95k, you should qualify for ~$188k comfortably. TRP Lending works directly with HomeGrown applicants.
→ TRP Lending: (312) 666-1323
✓
Gather income documents Required
Collect for all household adults 18+: last 3 pay stubs · 2 years federal tax returns + all schedules · 2 years W-2s · Social Security / pension year-end statements if applicable.
✓
Find a property & verify Zone A status Critical
Eligible types: condo, single-family, townhome, 2-flat. No ground-up new construction. Run every address through the city's Zone Lookup Tool before making an offer.
→ Open Zone A/B Lookup Tool
✓
Sign a purchase contract
You'll need a fully executed purchase contract to submit. Your $40k grant stays within the 25% cap at any price between $180k–$235k. Must plan to occupy for at least 5 years.
✓
Submit your application — act fast First-come basis
$21M total fund for ~300–400 buyers. Applications opened June 8, 2026. Apply through TRP Lending or Neighborhood Lending Services. Have every document ready before submitting.
→ Apply via The Resurrection Project   → chicago.gov/homegrown
✓
Have $2,300 personal contribution ready Required
At a $230k purchase price, you must contribute at least 1% ($2,300) from your own personal funds — not a gift or secondary loan. Required in addition to the grant.
